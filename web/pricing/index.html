Premium Vendor Plan

Loca Market · locamarket.in · Prices in Indian Rupees (INR)

Loca Market is a local marketplace where customers find nearby shops, items, prices and offers. Registering a shop is separate from this plan, and a shop stays live and visible with or without a plan. The Premium Vendor plan is the one paid plan for shop owners. It unlocks extra promotion services in the V Loca Market app.

Premium Vendor

36 months (3 years) per plan

The current price in Indian Rupees and the exact plan period are shown in the V Loca Market app before you pay. We may change the price for new plans; a plan you have already paid for keeps its price and period.

One-time payment for the period shown. There is no automatic renewal. The price includes all taxes that apply.

What it includes

  • Today’s Offer: put one item with a photo and an offer price in front of nearby customers for 24 or 48 hours. It goes live at once and you can post a new one when it ends.
  • Offer boxes on the home page: your offers take turns in the Today’s Offers boxes that customers in your area see.
  • Wholesale offers: see wholesale offers for shop owners, with minimum quantity and valid-until date.
  • Ads and other premium services that the Loca Market team marks as needing a plan.

How it works

  • Choose the Premium Vendor plan in the V Loca Market app and pay the price shown there.
  • The plan starts on the day the payment is confirmed and runs for the full period. If you buy again while a plan is running, the new period starts when the running plan ends.
  • You pay the price shown on the day you choose the plan, even if the price changes later.
  • When the plan ends, the premium services stop. Your shop, items and prices stay live as before.

Payment

You can pay online (UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking) through Razorpay, or by bank transfer. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. You get a payment confirmation in the app.
