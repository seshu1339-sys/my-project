Privacy Policy

Local Market · locamarket.in · Effective 4 November 2026

Download this policy as a PDF · Back to the shop

This policy explains what information Local Market collects when you use our website locamarket.in and our Android apps (Local Market for customers, V Local Market for shop owners and S Local Market for field staff), why we collect it, who sees it, and the choices you have. You can read it without signing in.

In short. We use your details to show you shops and offers near you, pass your order requests on to shops, and run the service. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use advertising networks. (Shops on Local Market may show their own ads in the app; those ads do not receive your personal details.) You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time at info@locamarket.in.

1. Who we are

Local Market is a local shopping service that connects customers with nearby shops and service providers. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “Local Market” mean the operator of locamarket.in and the apps listed above. Contact: info@locamarket.in.

2. Information we collect

If you shop as a customer

  • Account: your email address and a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password), and the name and language you choose.
  • Location: the town, pincode and address you choose, and, only if you allow it, your device’s approximate or precise location (GPS). We use it to show shops, products, offers and ads that are meant for your area, how far each shop selling an item is from you, and the road to a shop. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in your device or browser settings and choose a place by hand instead.
  • Order requests: when you send an order request, the items, your address and pincode, your phone number, and the status of the request. Your phone number is typed in for each request, is kept on that request only (not in your account) and is not checked with a code. Your location at that moment is used only to work out the distance to each shop for its delivery charge; we keep that distance and charge on the request, not your coordinates.
  • What Loca Market does not do with an order request: it only passes the request to the shop. We do not deliver anything and we do not take any money, neither for the items nor for the delivery charge. The payment and the handing over of the items are between you and the vendor, directly. We do not collect or store card, UPI or bank details for orders. If you choose to save a card for display, only the card brand and the last four digits are kept.
  • A wrong phone number: the vendor calls the number you give. If it is wrong and the vendor cannot reach you, the request may not go ahead. That is your own responsibility, and Loca Market is not responsible for it.
  • Things you save or write: your wishlist, reviews and ratings, and messages in complaints you send.
  • Notifications: if you turn notifications on, a device notification token and your chosen area, so we can send price drops and offers, and updates about your requests.

If you are a shop owner (Loca Market Vendor)

  • Your name, email, phone number, shop name, category, address, pincode and description.
  • The shop’s GPS location, a photo of you and a photo of the shop (kept private; seen only by you and our administrators while we review your application), and the shop photos, products, prices, stock, offers and ads you add.
  • Once we approve your shop, customers see its name, photo, address, phone number (so they can call you), location on the map, and your items and prices, so they can compare shops and find the way to you.
  • Photos: your shop photo (with the shop’s name board or you, the owner) and the extra photos your plan allows, including your own item photos, which our administrators check before they are shown. When you replace a photo, the old one is deleted; when a paid plan ends, extra photos beyond the free number are deleted.
  • With a paid plan (Silver, Gold or Premium), customers within your plan’s distance see your box in Today’s Offers and in the website’s ad boxes (the offer and the photo you choose); a tap shows your shop’s location, address and the way there. We use the customer’s location only to decide whether they are within that distance.
  • If one of our field staff helped you register, they may enter your Vendor ID in the staff app within 30 minutes of your registration (only staff whose area includes your shop can). We then record which staff member registered you (their Staff ID). That staff member sees only your shop name, your Vendor ID, how many items you have and when you last updated them. They cannot change your shop, items or prices.

If you offer a service (service provider, in the Loca Market Vendor app)

  • Your name, email, mobile number, a photo of you, the service you offer and your charge per hour, and your house address and GPS location (for our review).
  • Once we approve you, customers see your name, photo, service, charge per hour, mobile/WhatsApp number, your town and an approximate location (about 1 km) so they can find and call you. Your house address and exact location are not shown to customers.
  • If one of our field staff helped you register, they may enter your Provider ID in the staff app within 30 minutes of your registration. We then record which staff member registered you (their Staff ID). That staff member sees only your name, your Provider ID and when you last updated your listing. They cannot change it.

If you are field staff (Loca Market staff)

  • Your name, email, phone number and a registration photo, and the offers and rates you add for the shops in your area.
  • Live photos: each rate update needs a photo taken with the camera at the shop. With it we record your GPS location, how exact it is, the time, and whether the phone reported a mock (fake) location, to check that you were within 100 metres of the shop. Your work counts (photos, rate days, vendors you linked, missed days) are kept for your targets and our reports.
  • Your location (GPS) when you register, and the area and pincode worked out from it. Your Staff ID is made from that pincode. Only our administrators see this location.

Payments

  • Online payments to Loca Market (for example the Premium Vendor plan fee) are processed by Razorpay. You enter your card, UPI or bank details on Razorpay’s page, not in our apps. We do not collect or store your card, UPI or bank details. We keep the payment record Razorpay gives us: the amount, the status, the payment and payment-link reference numbers and the date, linked to your account.
  • What you receive for a payment is digital; see our Digital Delivery & Refund Policy.

Help chat (Flash) and support tickets

  • If you use the Flash help chat in an app, the messages you type, and details of your own account that the help needs (for example your plan, payments, offers or order requests), are sent to Anthropic, the company that runs the Claude AI model, so it can write a reply. The AI cannot change anything in your account, and it has no access to our database beyond what is sent with your message.
  • If Flash cannot answer, the chat becomes a support ticket. The text of your messages in that ticket is read by Claude to suggest to our administrator how to resolve it; the administrator decides and replies. We keep your chat messages and tickets, and a log of each AI call (which feature, the number of tokens used and your account id), and limit how many messages one person can send in a day.

Collected automatically

  • Visit and ad statistics: a random identifier created on your device (not linked to your name), whether you use the website or the app, your chosen town, and whether an ad was on screen for at least a second or was tapped. We use this to count visits and to report to advertisers how many people saw an ad. We do not count views by the advertiser themselves, our staff or automated programs.
  • How the apps are used: which screens and buttons are used, what is searched for (and searches that found nothing), calls, WhatsApp messages and directions to shops and service workers, offers and ads tapped, the town chosen, and whether a notification was opened. We keep these as daily counts. When you are signed in, we also keep your own daily counts, so our administrators can see how each account uses the service, and when your account was last active. Shop owners, service providers and staff see only the totals for their own shops, services and the vendors they added (for example, how many calls a shop got), never who made them.
  • App problems and speed: when an app has an error or crash, a short description of it (with emails, links and long numbers removed), and how long the app took to start.
  • Google Analytics (website): on our website, Google Analytics counts visits and the pages and buttons used, with a random identifier stored in your browser. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalisation, so it is not used for advertising or to guess your age or gender, and we do not send it your name, email, phone number or account id.
  • Local storage: your device or browser keeps small settings (language, chosen place, shopping bag, the random identifier above) so the service works the way you left it.

3. How we use information

  • To run the service: show products, shops, offers and ads for your area, pass your order requests to the shops, and show the status of your requests.
  • To check and approve shop owners, staff, photos and ads, and to keep the platform safe from fraud and misuse.
  • To send notifications you asked for, and messages about your order requests and complaints.
  • To count visits and ad views, report to advertisers, and improve the service.
  • To see which parts of the service are used, find what customers search for but cannot find, show shop owners, service providers and staff their own numbers, and find and fix errors and slow screens.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and order request information while your account is active and as long as needed for order requests, complaints, accounts and legal duties. Per-view ad records are kept so that advertisers’ numbers can be checked. Photos of shop owners and staff are kept while their account is active. Daily usage counts are kept as totals; your own daily counts are kept for up to 400 days. If a customer account is not used for a long time (for example 30 days), we may disable it, and if it then stays unused (for example 90 days in all), we may delete it with its personal information; order requests are kept as the law requires. Shop owner, service provider and staff accounts are not deleted this way. When you ask us to delete your information (section 8) we delete or anonymise it, except what we must keep by law.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to personal information is limited to the people and systems that need it, and administrator access needs a password and a one-time code. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Children

Local Market is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and change your name, language, address and saved details in your account. For anything else, write to us.
  • Deletion: see how to delete your account and data, or email info@locamarket.in from the address (or writing the mobile number) you registered with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and delete your account and personal information within 7 days, except what we must keep by law.
  • Location: turn it off in your device or browser settings at any time; you can still choose a place by hand.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your device settings. We count whether a notification was opened, to know which ones are useful.
  • Google Analytics: you can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or an add-on; the website works the same without it.
  • Complaints: write to us first; we will try to put things right.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will put the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is important.
