Delete your account and data

Local Market · locamarket.in · the Local Market, V Local Market and S Local Market apps and the website

This page applies to all three LocaMarket apps: Loca Market (customers), V Loca Market (shop owners and service providers) and S Loca Market (field staff), and to the website. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information we hold about you. You do not need to sign in to read this page or to make the request.

How to ask
  1. Send an email to info@locamarket.in from the email address you registered with, or write the mobile number you registered with in the email.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account, and say which app you used (customer, shop owner or field staff). Adding your name or shop name helps us find you.
  3. We reply to confirm it is you, then delete your data. We delete your data within 7 days and tell you when it is done.

Email us to delete my account

Want to remove only some data (for example your location or notifications)? Say so in the email, or switch them off yourself in your device settings.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in (email and password record).
  • Your personal details: name, phone number, email address, language, saved address, location (your saved place and the GPS location you gave at registration), wishlist, and the saved card display (brand and last four digits).
  • Your photos (your own photo, shop photos and service photos) and the items you added.
  • Your notification settings and device notification tokens.
  • Service providers: your application, your photo and your service listing.
  • Shop owners and field staff: your application details, your personal and shop photos, and the shop, products, offers and ads you added (or, if you prefer, we can keep the shop listing and remove only you as its owner).
  • Your reviews and ratings are removed or made anonymous.
