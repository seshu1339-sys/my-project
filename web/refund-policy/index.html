Refund & Cancellation Policy

Loca Market · locamarket.in · Effective 4 November 2026

In short. You can cancel the Premium Vendor plan for a full refund before it starts. Once it has started, the plan fee is not refunded. Payments taken twice, or taken for a plan that we could not start, are always refunded in full within 7 working days.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers payments made to Loca Market by shop owners and service providers: the Premium Vendor plan and the shop registration fee (when one is asked for). Loca Market does not sell items to customers or take payment for them. See also the Digital Delivery & Refund Policy.

2. Cancelling the Premium Vendor plan

  • Before the plan starts (for example, a plan bought to start after your running plan ends): you can cancel it and get a full refund.
  • After the plan has started: the plan gives you the premium services at once, so the fee is not refunded, in full or in part. The plan keeps running until the end of its period.
  • The plan does not renew by itself, so there is nothing to cancel at the end of a period.

3. When we always refund in full

  • You were charged more than once for the same plan or fee.
  • Money was taken from your account but the payment failed or the plan was not started within 3 working days.
  • We stop the Premium Vendor plan for everyone before your period ends. In that case we refund the part of the fee for the days that are left.

4. When we do not refund

  • Your account or shop was suspended because you broke our Terms & Conditions (for example, false items, prices or photos, or misleading offers).
  • You did not use the premium services during the plan period.

5. How to ask for a refund

Write to info@locamarket.in from the email you registered with, within 7 days of the payment, with your shop name, the payment date, amount and the payment or UPI reference number. We reply within 2 working days.

6. How the money comes back

Approved refunds are sent to the same account, card or UPI ID you paid from, within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. We do not refund in cash.

7. Contact

Questions about a payment or refund: info@locamarket.in · Contact Us
