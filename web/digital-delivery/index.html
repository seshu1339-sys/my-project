Loca Market · locamarket.in · Effective 4 November 2026

In short. Everything you pay for on Loca Market is digital. Nothing is shipped. The Premium Vendor plan is switched on in your account in the V Loca Market app, and a document you pay for on our website is sent as a PDF to the WhatsApp number or email you give. 1. What is delivered

We do not ship any physical goods. What we deliver is digital only:

  • Premium Vendor plan. When your payment is confirmed, the plan is activated on your shop owner or service provider account in the V Loca Market app. You can see it in the app. Details of the plan and its price are on the Premium Vendor Plan page.
  • Documents bought on our website. When your payment is successful, the document is made as a PDF file and sent to the WhatsApp number or the email address you gave when you paid.

2. Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay (UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking). We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. All prices are in Indian Rupees.

3. If you did not receive it

  • PDF document: check that the WhatsApp number or email you gave is correct, and look in your spam or junk folder.
  • Premium Vendor plan: close and open the V Loca Market app, and check that you are signed in to the same account you paid from.
  • If it is still missing, write to us at info@locamarket.in from your registered email (or WhatsApp us) with the payment date, amount and the payment reference number. We will send it again, or refund the payment if we cannot.

4. Refunds

  • Refunds of the Premium Vendor plan fee and of other payments made to Loca Market are handled by Loca Market itself.
  • When a payment is refunded, and in which cases, is set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • A payment that was taken twice, or taken but not followed by the plan or the PDF, is refunded.
  • Refund timeline: [REFUND TIMELINE – to be confirmed by Loca Market]
  • Refunds go back to the same account, card or UPI ID that was used to pay.

5. How to ask for a refund

Write to info@locamarket.in with the email or WhatsApp number you paid with, the payment date and amount, and the payment reference number.
